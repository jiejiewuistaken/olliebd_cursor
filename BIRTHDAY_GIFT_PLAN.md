# Birthday Gift Plan

This file records the gift concepts and how they should appear in the cinema
experience.

## 1. Black cat lamp

- Gift idea: DIY black cat lamp.
- Mood: warm, gentle, protective, like a small night companion across time zones.
- Visual direction:
  - black cat silhouette
  - warm amber light
  - moon/window/night motif
  - could feel like a quiet guardian cat
- Possible clue style:
  - "For the nights when the room needs a small familiar shadow."
  - "A little black cat that keeps watch when the cities are asleep."

## 2. Acrylic travel photo magnets

- Gift idea: acrylic magnets using travel photos she sent.
- Presentation idea: show them as a Europe map.
- Interaction / visual direction:
  - map of Europe as the base
  - travel photo magnets placed near their cities/countries
  - magnets look glass-like / acrylic
  - a glass-like highlight or object moves around the map, making the magnets shimmer
- Mood: her travels across Europe become collectible memories.
- Possible clue style:
  - "A map made from places you already turned into scenes."
  - "Small windows from the continent, waiting to become a constellation."

## 3. Film ticket coupons

- Gift idea: film ticket coupon set.
- Current status: leave blank for now.
- Future use:
  - can become "Admit Two" tickets
  - can be future promises, date coupons, call coupons, or trip coupons
  - should match the cinema ticket / film-frame visual language

## 4. Custom playlist as soundtrack

- Gift idea: custom playlist as the birthday soundtrack.
- In-app presentation:
  - this is the BGM / soundtrack of the cinema
  - show a gramophone record rolling/spinning in the right top corner
  - when user hovers on the record, show the song name
- Visual direction:
  - small vinyl/gramophone record
  - subtle spinning animation
  - compatible with dark cinema UI
  - hover label should feel like a tiny record-store tag or soundtrack caption
- Possible clue style:
  - "The soundtrack for the scenes we could not film together."
  - "Hover the record; every song is a small subtitle."

## 5. Voice-letter director's commentary

- Gift idea: voice-letter messages as "director's commentary."
- Count: three voice-letter moments.
- Presentation idea:
  - each one can be a hidden clue / unlockable scene
  - style like a director commentary track attached to the birthday movie
  - should feel intimate, cinematic, and personal
- Possible structure:
  1. Commentary 01: a memory / why this photo or moment matters.
  2. Commentary 02: a long-distance message / something encouraging.
  3. Commentary 03: a future-facing message / a promise or hope.
- Possible clue style:
  - "Commentary Track 01: play this when the scene feels quiet."
  - "A voice behind the screen, explaining the part that words missed."

## Overall design direction

- Keep everything compatible with the current cinema scene.
- Avoid ordinary web popups.
- Use cinematic objects:
  - film clue cards
  - ticket album stickers
  - map/magnets
  - gramophone record
  - director commentary tracks
- The experience should feel like collecting scenes from a birthday movie, not just
  opening a list of gifts.
